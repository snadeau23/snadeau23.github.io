3D Design

TV Yellow Guitar

What I often consider my dream guitar, this model is based off of a TV Yellow Gibson Les Paul Special. The culmination of months of on and off work, this model was completed in May of 2021 and clocks in at 186,084 vertices. This was intended as a way for me to practice my skills, and ended up being the work I am most proud of.

Skateboard & Ramp

This pair of models was originally intended to be entered into a Halloween render competition. In lieu of its completion, this skateboard and concrete ramp have become a hobby project I am particularly proud of. The skateboard is modeled after one of my own, including the same ATM deck graphic. The ramp includes stickers from Halloween themed bands and skateboard companies.

SmileDev Game Intros

These three animated intros were created for a game development team I was part of. All animated using Blender, The egg intro was for a game jam project called "Egg Rush," the pixelated intro for a game called "Tower of Towers," and the neon sign intro for an unreleased project.
