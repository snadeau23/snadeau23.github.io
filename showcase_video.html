Steven Nadeau

Video Projects

"A Short Day and a Very Long Night" is a mini video project about a group of friends from across the United States camping in the Wisconsin Dells. Recorded from July 4th - 9th 2023, and edited over July 11th - 12th. When filming, I had a very specific vision for the project involving unique still shots and framing that told our story. The concept of splitting the 6 days of filming into one day and one nighttime section came during the editing process, and reflects how the trip felt to me in immediate hindsight: a short day and one long night.

No Friendship is an Accident

"No Friendship is an Accident" is a video project about online friends coming together for the first time. It documents our experiences during the week of the 4th of July, 2022, in which our Canadian and Ohioan friends visited my friend group in Racine, Wisconsin.

Cezanne

"Cezanne" is a skate video filmed over my road trip to New England in August of 2022, and marks my second attempt at a road trip skate video. Filming a video like this was difficult for me, as I was on a time constraint to get clips of me skating. "Undoubtedly there are things in nature which have not yet been seen. If an artist discovers them, he opens the way for his successors."

Commodore Reserved

"Commodore Reserved" was my first road trip skate video, filmed over my 2021 road trip to New England on the last week of summer vacation. The video gets its namesake from the 1:20 clip of me skating in the parking spot of the yacht club's Commodore.
