Steven Nadeau

My name is Steven Nadeau, and I am a Science Communication, Astrophysics, and Digital Studies student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. As an advocate for science accessibility, I am passionate about making science understandable to everyone through digital media and an open-minded approach to outreach. As an aspiring science communicator, I aim to cut the jargon, making complex subjects not only digestible, but engaging and memorable by leveraging principles of universal design and storytelling, with technical media and strategy skills to back it up. Working in a variety of communications settings from directing photography for a student magazine to representing NASA at a national conference, my experience has shown me that communication isn't just about the message, but how you get there. My proof is the excitement I have for the world around me, all the way from skateboarding to space physics.
