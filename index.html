Steven Nadeau

My name is Steven Nadeau, and I am a Science Communication, Astrophysics, and Digital Studies student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. I am passionate about making science understandable to everyone through digital media and an open-minded approach to outreach. As an aspiring science communicator, I aim to cut the jargon, making complex subjects not only digestable and engaging by leveraging technical media and strategy skills to back it up. Working in a variety of communications settings from directing photography for a student magazine to representing NASA at a national conference, my experience has shown me that communication isn't just about the message, but how you get there.
