Steven Nadeau

Photography

Wisconsin Union

As photo/video marketing intern for the Wisconsin Union, I shoot a variety of events, occasions, and products by the Wisconsin Union. The goal of every photo is always to tell the story of the Union by capturing "memories that last a lifetime." Through the guidance of the marketing department, the Union has not only made me a better photographer, videographer, and editor, but a better visual storyteller.

Wisconsin Engineer Magazine

As head of photography for the Wisconsin Engineer Magazine, I direct the vision of the magazine by managing our student photography staff. We strive to tell STEM-focused stories both on and off-campus.

Astronomy

Astronomy has been a major theme of my photography career. I am passionate about capturing the emotions behind the science, which are at the very core of successful science communication and outreach. As a visual storyteller, I have found opportunities to take photos for the Charles & Kathryn Heide Observatory, the Astronomy Club at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, and for my own pleasure. By framing science in this way, my goal is to motivate self-learning and share the joy of the world above and around us.
